AICTE · PS 26264

Complaints are handled in separate Bureaus. The connections between them are not.

CaseSync surfaces evidence-backed relationships across Bureaus, flags coordination risks, and tracks authorised coordination through to a recorded outcome. It does not make regulatory decisions.

1The problem

An institution's case history is scattered across Bureaus. Related complaints go unnoticed; deadlines and dependencies collide.

2What CaseSync does

Resolves institution identity, suggests related cases with visible evidence, flags gaps and conflicts, and runs a human-controlled workflow to a recorded outcome.

3Why you can trust it

Explainable suggestions, human review, role-based access, hash-chained audit and measurable evaluation. Nothing happens automatically.

Prototype with synthetic data. No AICTE system is connected.
